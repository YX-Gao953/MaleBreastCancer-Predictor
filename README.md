# Male_Breast_Cancer-predictor

A machine-learning based model to predict and stratify the overall survival of male patients with luminal early breast cancer.

Citation:  
Gao Y, Xu M, Zhang M, Sun G, Ye G, Nie J, Ma L, Chen W, Jiang Z, Lin Y. Development and validation of an online prognostic risk stratification tool to assist decisions on perioperative chemotherapy for men with luminal early breast cancer. Breast Cancer Res. 2026 Mar 28;28(1):80. doi: 10.1186/s13058-026-02252-5. PMID: 41904588; PMCID: PMC13151119.
